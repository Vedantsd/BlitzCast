Forecasting Challenge

Why Weather Forecasting Needs Smarter Blending

No single forecasting model performs best everywhere. Each model paradigm has distinct physical or statistical strengths and critical blind spots.

📐

NWP Models

ECMWF IFS, GFS, WRF

Ground-truth fluid dynamics and thermodynamic equations. Outstanding for short-range synoptic boundary physics.

Limitation: High computational latency; struggles with localized sub-grid tropical convection.
🧠

AI/ML Weather Models

ECMWF AIFS, GraphCast

Lightning-fast global inference trained on ERA5 reanalysis. Excels at recognizing large-scale flow patterns at medium range.

Limitation: Can smooth out extreme tail peaks; lacks explicit physics constraints during rare regime shifts.
📊

Ensemble Forecasts

50-Member Multi-Physics EPS

Quantifies atmospheric chaotic spread. Superior at generating reliable probabilistic distributions beyond Day 5.

Limitation: Ensemble averaging dampens high-intensity localized precipitation gradients.
🛰️

Historical Observations

IMD Radars & Rain Gauges

Essential ground truth validation verifying past forecast skill to recalibrate continuous learning weights.

Limitation: Ground networks have spatial density gaps across rugged mountain and maritime regions.
